# Digital Product Refunds

Refunds for digital products are available within **14 days** of purchase if
the download was not completed or the license key was unused.

- Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within
  **5–7 business days**.
- Partial refunds may apply when only some seats or add-ons are cancelled.
- Chargebacks should be avoided; contact support with order id `ORD-…` first.

Contact billing support with your order number to start a refund review.
